Paintless Dent Repair cost guide

How much does door ding repair cost?

Door dings from parking lots are the most common job for a paintless dent repair tech. When the paint isn't cracked, the tech pushes the metal back from behind the panel, so there is no sanding or repainting. The first dent costs the most. Each extra dent on the same visit usually costs less, so it pays to fix them all at once.

🛠 Do it yourself

Do it yourself or hire a pro?

Glue-pull dent kits can improve shallow, round dings on flat panels. Practice on a junk panel first, and don't use them on cracked or chipped paint. Ding repair is a skill, and a bad pull can make a dent worse. For dings on body lines or edges, a pro is the safer choice.

Dent technicians near you

Looking for dent technicians near you…

What's usually included

  • Each ding checked under a light board to confirm PDR will work
  • Door panels or trim removed when needed to reach behind the dent
  • Metal massaged back into shape with rods and tabs
  • Panels and trim put back and the area cleaned

What changes the price

  • Number of dings. Extra dents on the same visit usually cost less each.
  • Size. Larger and deeper dents take more time.
  • Location. Dents on body lines or panel edges are harder to fix.
  • Access. Some spots need trim or lights removed first.

Ways to save

  • Ask for a price for all your dings at once instead of one at a time.
  • Ask the tech whether each ding is a good fit for PDR before work starts.
  • Many PDR techs are mobile and can fix dings at your home or work.

Common questions

Will paintless dent repair work on my ding?

If the paint isn't cracked or chipped and the tech can reach the back of the panel, usually yes.

How long does it take?

A few small dings often take under an hour total.

Will it show afterward?

Most door dings can be fixed so they are hard to see. Very sharp creases may leave a faint mark.
