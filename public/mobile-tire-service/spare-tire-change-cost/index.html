Mobile Tire Service cost guide

How much does spare tire change cost?

If you have a flat and a usable spare, a mobile tire tech or roadside service can come to you and swap it on. It's a quick job, usually priced as a flat call-out. Larger trucks and SUVs, and wheels 20 inches and up, may cost more. A temporary spare is meant for short distances, so plan to repair or replace the flat soon.

🛠 Do it yourself

Do it yourself or hire a pro?

Changing to your spare yourself takes the jack and lug wrench that came with your car. Park on flat, solid ground away from traffic, loosen the lug nuts before lifting, and tighten them in a star pattern. Check the spare's air pressure every few months so it's ready when you need it.

Mobile tire techs near you

Looking for mobile tire techs near you…

What's usually included

  • A tech sent to your car
  • The car jacked up safely and the flat removed
  • Your spare checked for air and mounted
  • Lug nuts tightened and the flat stored in your trunk

What changes the price

  • Vehicle. SUVs, trucks and large wheels take more time and equipment.
  • Location. Highways and remote areas can add a travel fee.
  • Spare condition. A low or flat spare needs air first.
  • Locking lug nuts. A missing lug key slows the job down.
  • Coverage. Insurance and auto clubs often cover a spare swap.

Ways to save

  • Check your insurance and auto club before you pay. Many cover spare swaps.
  • Keep your locking lug nut key in the glove box.
  • Ask whether the tech can patch the flat on the spot instead, if the damage is in the tread.

Common questions

How far can I drive on a temporary spare?

Most compact spares are meant for short distances at lower speeds. Check the label on the spare or your owner's manual.

Can they fix my flat instead?

Many mobile tire techs can plug and patch a puncture in the tread on the spot, so you don't need the spare at all.

What if my car has no spare?

Many newer cars come with a repair kit instead. A mobile tire tech can repair the tire or bring a replacement.
