Car Detailing cost guide

How much does interior car detailing cost?

An interior detail cleans the inside of your car well past a quick vacuum. The price depends on the size of the vehicle and how dirty it is. Pet hair and stains are the most common reasons a quote goes up.

Typical price
$55 – $125
Most people pay around $90. About 1.5 hours of work.

Labor and basic supplies. Big parts or appliances you buy yourself are extra. Prices run higher in big cities.

What's usually included

  • Full vacuum of seats, carpets, mats and trunk
  • Dash, console, door panels and cup holders wiped and dressed
  • Interior glass cleaned
  • Floor mats washed
  • Vents and seams cleaned with brushes

What changes the price

  • Pet hair. It weaves into carpet and fabric and takes extra tools and time to pull out.
  • Seat type. Shampooing cloth seats takes longer than wiping and conditioning leather.
  • Third rows. Minivans and large SUVs have more seats and carpet to clean.
  • Odors and stains. Smoke, spills and mildew may need extra treatment.

Ways to save

  • Remove trash and personal items before the detailer arrives.
  • Vacuum out loose pet hair yourself first if your car is very furry. It can shorten the job.
  • Use seat covers and rubber mats afterward to make the next clean easier.

Do it yourself or hire a pro?

You can do a solid interior clean with a shop vac, brushes and an interior cleaner. What pros add is steam cleaning, extraction for stains and a lot of patience in the small gaps. For pet hair or set-in stains, a pro usually gets a better result.

Common questions

How long does an interior detail take?

Usually two to three hours for a typical car, and longer for large or very dirty vehicles.

Can detailing remove smoke smell?

It can reduce it a lot. Strong smoke odor often needs an ozone or enzyme treatment, which may cost extra.

Does an interior detail include shampooing seats?

Many do for cloth seats, but some only include it in higher packages. Ask before booking.
