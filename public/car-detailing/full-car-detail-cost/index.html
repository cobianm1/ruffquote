How much does full car detail cost?

A full detail covers both the interior and exterior, usually with a coat of wax. It takes several hours, so it costs a lot more than a wash. Vehicle size and condition set most of the price.

Detailers near you

Looking for detailers near you…

What's usually included

  • Hand wash and dry, including wheels and tires
  • Wax or sealant applied to the paint
  • Full interior vacuum and wipe-down
  • Windows cleaned inside and out
  • Tires dressed and floor mats cleaned

What changes the price

  • Paint correction. Polishing out swirls and scratches is a separate, longer service.
  • Ceramic coating. Coatings cost much more than wax and are priced on their own.
  • Engine bay. Engine cleaning is often an add-on.
  • Water access. Mobile detailers without a hookup may need to bring their own tank.

Ways to save

  • Get a full detail a couple of times a year and do simple washes in between.
  • Skip paint correction unless your paint has visible swirls you want gone.
  • Ask a mobile detailer if they offer a discount for two cars at the same address.

Do it yourself or hire a pro?

A careful home wash, wax and interior clean gets you much of the way there. It takes most of a day and the right products. Pros are faster and better at the hard parts, like stain removal and even wax coverage.

Common questions

How long does a full detail take?

Most take three to five hours, depending on the vehicle and how dirty it is.

Is a full detail worth it before selling a car?

It often helps. A clean car photographs better and makes a stronger first impression on buyers.

What is the difference between wax and ceramic coating?

Wax is a quick protective layer that lasts weeks to months. Ceramic coatings last much longer but cost more and take more prep.
