Car Detailing cost guide

How much does detailing a car before you sell it cost?

A clean car photographs better and makes a better first impression on buyers. Before a sale, most people book a full detail: the outside washed, clayed and waxed, and the inside vacuumed, shampooed and wiped down. Pet hair and smoke odor take extra work. For a trade-in, an interior detail alone may be enough.

Typical price
$105 – $230
Most people pay around $170. About 3 hours of work.

Detailers near you

Looking for detailers near you…

What's usually included

  • Hand wash, wheels and tires cleaned
  • Clay bar and a coat of wax or sealant
  • Full vacuum, including trunk and under the seats
  • Seats and carpets shampooed or leather cleaned
  • Dash, doors, glass and vents wiped down

What changes the price

  • Vehicle size. SUVs, trucks and vans take longer.
  • Condition. Pet hair, stains and smoke odor add time.
  • Package. A full detail costs more than inside or outside alone.
  • Extras. Engine bay cleaning and headlight restoration cost more.

Ways to save

  • Book the detail a day or two before you take listing photos.
  • Ask about headlight restoration if your lights look yellow.
  • Ask for odor treatment if anyone smoked in the car.

Common questions

Is detailing worth it before selling?

Often, yes. A clean car makes a better first impression and stands out in photos.

Should I detail before a trade-in?

A quick interior clean is usually enough. Dealers recondition cars themselves.

How long does a full detail take?

Usually a few hours, more for large or very dirty vehicles. The calculator above shows an estimate.
