Tree Trimming cost guide

How much does trimming several trees cost?

Tree crews charge for their time, their equipment and hauling the brush away. Getting the truck, chipper and crew to your house is part of every job, so trimming several trees in one visit costs less per tree than calling them out each time. Height matters most. Trees over the house or near power lines need more care.

🛠 Do it yourself

Do it yourself or hire a pro?

Small trees under about 15 feet are a fair DIY job with loppers, a pruning saw and a pole saw from the ground. Avoid climbing with a chainsaw, and leave anything near power lines to a pro. Trim in late winter for most trees, when they are dormant and you can see the branches.

Tree trimmers near you

Looking for tree trimmers near you…

What's usually included

  • Dead, broken and crossing branches cut back
  • Branches thinned and shaped for each tree
  • Branches kept off the roof, driveway and walkways
  • Brush chipped and hauled away, unless you ask to keep it

What changes the price

  • Number of trees. More trees per visit lowers the cost per tree.
  • Height. Tall trees need climbing gear or a bucket truck.
  • Location. Trees over the house or near lines need extra care.
  • Cleanup. Chipping and hauling cost more than leaving the wood stacked.
  • Access. Backyard trees behind fences may need more hand carrying.

Ways to save

  • Walk the yard with the crew and point out every tree you want trimmed.
  • Ask for a price with haul-away and with the wood left stacked.
  • Ask neighbors if they want trees done the same day. Some crews give a discount.

Common questions

Is it cheaper to trim all my trees at once?

Usually, yes. Setup and travel are spread across more trees, so the price per tree goes down.

When is the best time to trim trees?

Late winter is best for most trees. Dead or dangerous branches can be cut any time.

How long will it take?

It depends on the number and size of the trees. The calculator above shows an estimate of crew hours for your job.
