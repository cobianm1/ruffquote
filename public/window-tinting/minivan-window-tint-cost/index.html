Window Tinting cost guide

How much does tinting a minivan or large SUV cost?

Minivans and large SUVs have more windows and more glass than a sedan, including big sliding-door windows and wide rear glass. That means more film and more labor, so the price runs higher. Many families choose tint to keep kids cooler and block sun in the back rows. Ceramic film costs more but blocks more heat without going darker.

Typical price
$235 – $440
Most people pay around $340. About 3.5 hours of work.

🛠 Do it yourself

Do it yourself or hire a pro?

Tinting a whole minivan yourself is a big job, especially the large curved back glass. If you want to try, start with a pre-cut kit and the flat side windows. Many owners tint the side windows themselves and leave the back glass to a shop.

Tint installers near you

Looking for tint installers near you…

What's usually included

  • Glass cleaned inside and the door seals wiped down
  • Film cut for each window, including sliding-door and quarter glass
  • Film heat-shrunk to fit curved rear glass
  • Film applied and squeegeed with no bubbles at the edges
  • Care instructions for the first few days

What changes the price

  • Number of windows. Minivans and large SUVs have more glass to cover.
  • Film. Ceramic film costs more but blocks more heat.
  • Rear glass. Large curved back windows take more skill.
  • Old tint. Removing old film adds time.
  • Factory glass. Dark factory rear glass may only need a light film or none.

Ways to save

  • Ask whether the price includes the small quarter windows.
  • If your rear windows already have factory privacy glass, ask about tinting only the front windows.
  • Compare carbon and ceramic film prices for your vehicle.

Common questions

Why does a minivan cost more to tint?

There are more windows and more glass, including large sliding-door windows and a wide back window.

Is ceramic film worth it in a family car?

It blocks more heat and UV without going darker, which many parents like for the back rows. It costs more than dyed or carbon film.

How long does it take?

Usually a few hours for a full minivan. The calculator above shows an estimate.
