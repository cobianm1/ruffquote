What affects the price of gutter cleaning

  • Length. Most pros price by the linear foot of gutter.
  • Height. Two- and three-story homes take longer and carry more risk.
  • How full they are. Gutters skipped for years can take twice as long.
  • Roof pitch and gutter guards. Steep roofs and guards slow the job down.

The low end of the range is a newer gutter cleaner at about $35 an hour. The high end is an experienced pro at about $70 an hour who travels to you. Many gutter cleaners have a minimum charge of around $100. Prices are higher in big cities.

Common questions

How often should gutters be cleaned?

Twice a year, in late spring and late fall. Homes under big trees may need it more.

Is it worth paying for?

For two-story homes, usually yes. Ladder falls are a common cause of injuries at home.

How the prices are worked out

Each price is your hourly rate times the typical time for that job, plus supplies and driving, plus your overhead and profit, rounded up to the nearest $5. Small jobs use a $100 minimum. If you work faster or slower than the times shown, adjust your hourly rate.

Before you post prices, check two or three local gutter cleaners on Google or Facebook. If you're well below them, you can probably charge more.
