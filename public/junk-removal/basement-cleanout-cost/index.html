Junk Removal cost guide

How much does basement cleanout cost?

Junk removal companies price by how much of their truck your stuff fills. Basements cost more than garages because everything has to be carried up the stairs. Heavy items like old appliances, exercise machines and furniture add to the price. Sorting what you want to keep before the crew arrives saves time and money.

🛠 Do it yourself

Do it yourself or hire a pro?

If you have a truck and helpers, you can haul it yourself and pay only dump fees. Sort into keep, donate and toss piles first, and take donations to a thrift store. A rented dumpster is another option for a big cleanout, but someone still has to carry everything up the stairs.

Junk haulers near you

Looking for junk haulers near you…

What's usually included

  • A crew to carry everything up the stairs and out
  • Loading and hauling to the dump, donation or recycling
  • A sweep of the cleared area
  • Pricing confirmed on site before work starts

What changes the price

  • Volume. The share of the truck your stuff fills sets the base price.
  • Stairs. Narrow or steep basement stairs add labor.
  • Heavy items. Appliances, pianos and gym equipment cost extra.
  • Special items. Paint, tires and mattresses may carry disposal fees.
  • Water damage. Wet or moldy items may need extra handling.

Ways to save

  • Sort and set aside what you are keeping before the crew arrives.
  • Ask whether the price includes all disposal fees.
  • Ask whether usable items are donated rather than dumped.

Common questions

Why does a basement cost more than a garage?

Everything has to go up the stairs, which takes more time and care than rolling items out a garage door.

Can they take old paint and chemicals?

Many companies can't take hazardous waste. Check with your local waste program for drop-off days.

How long does a basement cleanout take?

It depends on how much there is and the stairs. Ask the crew for an estimate when they see the space.
